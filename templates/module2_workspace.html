How the Step-Grid works

Each card is one fraction. The numerator goes on top and the denominator goes on the bottom.

Build the chain one step at a time: the denominator unit must cancel the numerator unit carried from the previous step, until only the target unit remains.

Verify each step with Verify Step. Hints are always available and never lower your score.

The Diagonal Unit Cancellation Method
1. The Diagonal Cancellation Rule

Units cancel out diagonally across fractions.
• Put the unit you want to CANCEL on the BOTTOM (Denominator).
• Put the unit you want to REACH on the TOP (Numerator).

2. Molar Mass Cards (g ↔ mol)

Converts between mass and moles of the same substance.
• Always pair the periodic molar mass (in grams) with 1 mol!
(e.g., 18.015 g / 1 mol or 1 mol / 18.015 g)
