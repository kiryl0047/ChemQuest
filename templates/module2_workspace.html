The Diagonal Unit Cancellation Method
1. The Diagonal Cancellation Rule

Units cancel out diagonally across fractions.
• Put the unit you want to CANCEL on the BOTTOM (Denominator).
• Put the unit you want to REACH on the TOP (Numerator).
